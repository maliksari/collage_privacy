Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Moxie Collage does not collect, store or share your personal data.

Photos and videos

You choose photos and videos with the system picker. They are processed only on your device and are never uploaded.

AI cutout

Background removal uses Apple’s on-device Vision framework. No data leaves your device.

Analytics

Optional usage analytics are off by default. If enabled, events stay in your device’s local log and are never sent to us or third parties.

Purchases

The app has no in-app purchases.

Third parties

No advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs.

Data storage

Projects are stored in the app’s container on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Updates will be posted on this page.
