FAQ

Does the app need access to all my photos?
No. You pick only the photos you want; nothing else is visible to the app.

Where are my collages saved?
Drafts stay in the app. Export to save to Photos or share.

Save to Photos fails.
Allow “Add Photos Only” in Settings › Moxie Collage › Photos.

AI cutout finds no subject.
Use a photo with a clear person, animal or object in front.

Is my data uploaded?
No. See the Privacy Policy.
